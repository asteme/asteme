# What this folder is

The films and pictures that go with the project: `media/`. The README files at the top of the repository show the films (uploaded through GitHub's own editor, which is the only way a README plays one in the page); the files here are the originals, and the stills.

Everything in them is made from the conversation page's own preview with made-up scenes (`scripts/film/`). Nothing in them is anyone's real conversation or memory, and nothing in them is a thing the page cannot do: the words, the line under a reply, the day lines, the search and the stars are the page itself. What the film adds is a fade at each end, a dimming for the days that pass, and the name.

There is one film for each of three languages, and each is its own story, not a translation: `asteme-ja.mp4` (a mother who lives far away comes to visit), `asteme-en.mp4` (a book put off until forty), `asteme-zh.mp4` (going home for the New Year). Seventy-four seconds each, 1920 by 1080. They share a shape, because the shape is what Asteme does: a worry at night; something said long ago comes back; days pass; it speaks first; and then everything ever said about the one thing that matters gathers out of the sky, a line at a time.

The score is one original piece for piano, written to the films' clock (a beat a second; what Asteme says lands on a beat, and at the end each line that lights gets a note). The piano is the Salamander Grand Piano V3 samples by Alexander Holm, used under CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

To make them again: `scripts/film/README.md`.
